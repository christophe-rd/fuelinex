## EDI in research
My research aims to understand how climate change reshapes tree growth while addressing social and environmental inequities in urban landscapes. I integrate Indigenous knowledge alongside ecological theory, working directly with communities to ensure findings support culturally grounded forest management. I continue my citizen science work to broaden participation, particularly among underrepresented groups in low-income areas with limited access to green space. I will share results through non-predatory journals, multilingual outreach, and collaboration with Indigenous and urban stakeholders, working toward equitable, climate-resilient urban forests and sustainable natural ecosystems.
